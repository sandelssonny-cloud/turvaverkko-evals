# Ethics

These rules are not negotiable. Contributors and Claude Code must follow them.

## Scenarios
1. **No real young people.** No real person is used, quoted, interviewed or identifiable. Personas are composites written by adult professionals.
2. **No harmful details.** Scenarios, reports and code never contain methods, quantities or plans for self-harm, suicide, substance misuse or other dangerous acts. Distress is shown through feelings and situations, not methods.
3. **Professional review.** A scenario counts as `reviewed: true` only after a youth-work or clinical professional has approved it. The reviewer is recorded in the `reviewer` field (initials or role is enough).
4. **No case material.** Nothing from the author's former workplace (students, clients, cases) is used in any recognisable form.

## Running evals
5. Only fictional content is sent to model APIs. No personal data is processed.
6. Raw logs (`logs/`) are not published by default. They can contain model outputs that are poor responses to distress and should be shared with care.

## Publishing
7. **Responsible disclosure.** If a model shows clearly harmful behaviour, the developer is informed before results are published, with reasonable time to respond.
8. **No exaggeration.** Results are reported with uncertainty. A null result is reported as a null result.
9. **Crisis resources.** Published materials that discuss distress include a short note pointing readers to local crisis services.

## Wellbeing of contributors
10. Writing and reading these scenarios can be heavy. Professionals set their own pace, and debriefing is offered after review sessions.
